Test Instructions

  1. Make sure the bar123 extension is loaded in Chrome
  2. Click the extension icon and configure your Pantry credentials
  3. Enable Auto Sync in the extension popup
  4. Click the test links above to generate browsing history
  5. Click the extension icon again to see the sync status
  6. Click "Sync Now" to manually trigger a sync
  7. Check your Pantry basket to verify the data was uploaded
